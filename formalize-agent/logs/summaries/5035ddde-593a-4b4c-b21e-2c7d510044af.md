* `RegGrowth.chain_lower_bound` (a chain of `k` loops gives `Ω(n^k)` accepted words) and `RegGrowth.accCount_le_of_not_chain` (no ambiguous cycle and no chain of `k+1` loops gives `O(n^k)`), the latter by induction on the size of the reachable set, with the forced path `loopWord`/`loopState` inside a strongly connected component and the decomposition `loopWord_decomp`.
* `regular_growth_dichotomy` — the growth gap theorem for regular languages.
