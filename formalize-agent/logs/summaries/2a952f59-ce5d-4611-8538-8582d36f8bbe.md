Thanks for the pointer — you were right that the LaTeX proof of Theorem C.1.4 goes through weighted automata, and the Lean development did not follow that route. It now does: the book's proof (reduction to zeroness of weighted automata over ℚ via the prime decomposition, with the map reverse and map duplicate constructions) is formalised and proved, with no `sorry` and no new axioms (`#print axioms` reports only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`). The whole project still builds.

Three new files in `transducer-lean/RequestProject/PartC/`:

* `WeightedLin.lean` — the converse of the existing "weighted automaton ⟹ linear representation": a function given by a linear representation, with an arbitrary initial row vector, is computed by a weighted automaton (`Transducers.WLin.isWeighted_lval`), plus closure of weighted automata over a ring under differences (`Transducers.WLin.isWeighted_sub`), which is the book's remark that equivalence reduces to zeroness. This lets all later constructions be done on matrices instead of on runs.
* `WeightedMapLift.lean` — the two prime constructions. What they have in common is isolated as the property that the matrix of a block `g v` is a fixed linear combination of the entries of the matrix of `v` in an auxiliary representation; for reverse the auxiliary matrices are the transposes (running a block backwards, and exactly where commutativity of the semiring is used, as the book notes), for duplicate they are the Kronecker squares (so a new transition weight is a product of two old ones, as the book says the reader should check). The new state space is `Q × P × P`, matching the triples of states in the book's pictures.
* `WeightedRegClosure.lean` — the reduction itself.

Results proved (all registered in the properties table):

* `Transducers.isWeighted_comp_mapReverse`, `Transducers.isWeighted_comp_mapDuplicate` — weighted automata over a commutative semiring are closed under pre-composition with map reverse and map duplicate.
* `Transducers.isWeighted_comp_regular` — the induction over the prime decomposition: weighted automata are closed under pre-composition with every regular function (the rational primes come from Lemma B.3.5, already in the project).
* `Transducers.exists_injective_weighted` — observation (a) of the book: over a finite alphabet, an injective map from strings to ℚ computed by a weighted automaton.
* `Transducers.regularFun_eq_iff_weighted_eq` and `Transducers.regularFun_eq_iff_weighted_zero` — the reduction as drawn in the book, in equivalence form and in zeroness form (a single weighted automaton over ℚ that vanishes identically iff the two regular functions are equal).
* `Transducers.regularFun_eq_of_short` — the resulting decision procedure in semantic form: two regular functions over finite alphabets are equal as soon as they agree on the finitely many inputs up to a bound coming from the zeroness criterion for weighted automata over a field.
