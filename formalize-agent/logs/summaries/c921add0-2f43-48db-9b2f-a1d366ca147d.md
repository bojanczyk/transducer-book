**Exercise `exer:rational-one-letter-input`** (`rational-functions.tex`) — `Transducers.Exercises.rationalFun_unary_graph` in `RequestProject/Exercises/PartBC.lean`: the graph of a rational function whose input alphabet has one letter is a finite union of sets `{ (a^(α+βk), x yᵏ z) | k ∈ ℕ }`. The proof is the author's: a bimachine exists by Theorem `thm:bimachines` (reused, not restated), its prefix and suffix automata over a one-letter alphabet have eventually periodic runs, and each further period inserts one more group of gaps in the middle producing the same piece of output. That analysis is in the new file `RequestProject/Exercises/PartBCUnary.lean`. Formalisation choices, recorded in the docstring: the one-letter input alphabet is `Unit`, the union is indexed by `Fin n`, `yᵏ` is `(List.replicate k y).flatten`, and the output alphabet is finite, as the book assumes.
